## Why this order

Every advisor (Lineup, Waivers, Trades, Front Office decision cards) consumes the shared player evaluation, so the Model is the critical path.

Timing: FFA is in scoring period 9 of 14 regular-season periods (Monday night of NFL week 9). The trade deadline is 2026-12-02 10:00 (UTC−7), and lineup and waiver advice stay useful through the playoffs.

The order follows from that:

1. The interpretable Baseline A ships first, weekly and then rest of season. Rest of season is what trades need. Slices 2–6 are sized to fit before the deadline; that is a target, not a promise, and each slice's gate still holds.
2. Machine-learning candidates follow and replace the baseline only where the gate says so.
3. The draft horizon comes last; the next draft is in August.

## Slice 1: FFA points from nflverse statistics

**Scope.**
1. `arbit_data.nflverse.stat_lines` (provider parsing stays in the data layer) turns one nflverse weekly player row into canonical FFA metrics (`passing.yards`, `kicking.field_goals_0_39`, ...). Where the weekly table lumps categories together, it adds that game's play-by-play. Each metric records the columns or play rule it came from. A metric the source can't supply is `None`, never zero, so the shared scorer refuses it (`MissingStatError`).
2. Team defense (D/ST) lines come from team stats, schedules and play-by-play. Points allowed and yards allowed follow the definition ESPN actually uses, settled against ESPN's raw values rather than assumed.
3. Scoring stays in `arbit_core.scoring.score` with the stored FFA `RuleSet`; nothing re-implements arithmetic.
4. The verification script `scripts/model/verify_nflverse_scoring.py` reads ESPN's raw per-category statistics for completed scoring periods through the existing paced, cookie-free client. It maps each ESPN player to a GSIS ID through stored aliases, compares every metric and the total with the nflverse line, and writes a research note and a machine-readable receipt.

**Evidence** ([research](../../research/nflverse-ffa-scoring-2026-11-09.md), [handoff](../../project/handoffs/2026-11-09-m3-ffa-points.md), ENG-051):
- Matched in every category: 7,171 of 7,202 compared player-weeks (2025) and 3,549 of 3,569 (2026).
- Source-side differences are R-060. Unattributed two-point returns stay unknown (R-061). Two players lack a GSIS alias (R-062).
- One change of method: verification read ESPN's per-week player pool instead of each period's rosters. That covered every player at every FFA position and the 2025 season, and settled more definitions.

## Slice 2: field catalog, availability policy, historical targets

**Scope.**
1. **Retrospective availability ADR (Q-005), adopted before any backtest.** For each nflverse family it states when a value counts as known. The plan proposed two decision times per game, before and after inactives. The measured vintages showed inactives reach nflverse only after the game (R-064), so ADR-007 uses one, at kickoff.

   It covers:
   - **Statistics and play-by-play:** published overnight after game days, with stat corrections Monday to Wednesday.
   - **Pre-game status:** injury and practice reports; weekly roster status (`INA` only after inactives); timestamped depth charts from 2025.
   - **Lagged sources:** snap counts; Next Gen Stats; PFR advanced; FTN charting with its lag; participation, which is earlier seasons only.
   - **Schedules:** pre-game fields. Recorded weather is history for later decisions (ADR-007 A2). The forecast for the upcoming game comes from the weather intelligence system (ADR-008). Lines follow OWN-067 (study, then ablation).
   - **Fitted models:** nflfastR model outputs, with the seasons the producing `fastrmodels` version trained on.

   Observed vintages since 2026-10-31 are real evidence. Older seasons use the labeled retrospective rule.
2. **Field catalog** (`libs/arbit-model`, machine-readable). It gives each of the 1,802 imported fields a use (pre-game, history, earlier seasons only, outcome, identity, excluded) with a reason and, where relevant, the first season it exists. A test fails when an imported manifest has a field without an entry, so new upstream fields can't be ignored silently.
3. **Historical targets.** FFA stat lines and scores per player-game and D/ST team-game, 2012–2026, built by the slice 1 adapter and the shared scorer. They are stored as versioned analytical tables with manifests (ADR-002) and carry unknown codes.
4. **Participation labels** from snap counts (2013 on; the import has no 2012 file) and weekly rosters: played with snaps, active without snaps, inactive, not on the roster. These separate "played, no recorded statistic" from "did not play". Verification found 1,464 ESPN played-weeks with no nflverse row, all zero.
5. **R-061.** Rule out players with no defensive or special-teams snap from unattributed two-point returns. The remaining ambiguity stays unknown.
6. **Model package.** `libs/arbit-model` becomes a workspace member, holding catalog, targets and later features, with no runtime service yet.
7. **Signal study.** `scripts/model/ffa_signal_study.py` stays reproducible; its constants seed Baseline A, which the backtest then confirms or replaces.

**Exclusions.** No features beyond targets and labels, no fitted model, API or UI. No change to slice 1 definitions.

**Acceptance.**
- Catalog test: every field of every current manifest is classified.
- Targets for 2025 and 2026 reproduce slice 1 verification scores exactly.
- Participation labels agree with snap counts on a sample of known DNPs and active zero-stat games.
- The availability ADR is accepted by the owner before slice 3 features are used in any backtest.
- Gates: `make runtime-check`; foundation validator.

**Migration and data impact.** Analytical Parquet tables and manifests in the object store; no relational migration expected. If one is needed, it goes to a `_test` copy first and is released only on the owner's request.

**Outcome (2026-11-10)** ([handoff](../../project/handoffs/2026-11-09-m3-slice2-catalog-targets.md)):
- **Package:** `libs/arbit-model` is a workspace member.
- **Field catalog** (`arbit_model.fields`, committed `nflverse_fields.json`): 1,827 fields in 32 families. `misc` is split by table because its columns share names.
  - Uses: 148 pre-game, 717 history, 18 earlier seasons only, 5 outcome, 411 identity, 528 excluded.
  - `scripts/model/field_catalog.py` passes against all 1,140 current manifests.
- **Targets** (`arbit_model.targets`, `scripts/model/build_targets.py`), 2012–2026:

  - 7,402 D/ST team-games, all scored.
  - 93,238 explicit zero lines for players who played without a statistics row. These are for every listed position, because a game's snap position isn't a player's FFA position.

Values fetched late after a capture gap count from the source's own timestamp or the family rule, never before the gap's start. Every field gets a time it applies, not a ban.

## Slice 2b: NFL availability, twice a day (OWN-063)

Owner direction after slice 2: NFL supplies inactives, did-not-play and weekly game status, read twice a day, every day (OWN-063). NFL's API has no inactives, so the owner then chose ESPN's fantasy injury status for game day (OWN-064).

**Implemented (2026-11-10, not released):**
- `arbit_data.availability`:
  - `sources`: the NFL injury report, paged with `pageToken`.
  - `espn_status`: ESPN's status for every pool player as of a moment, from the stored league documents. It is current only if Arbit read ESPN within 15 minutes; otherwise game-day availability is unknown.
  - `capture`: original bytes, the parsed game-status table, and a manifest. Its `observedAt` is when the reads finished. `captures.jsonl` lists the captures.
  - `schedule`: 12:00 and 19:15 America/New_York.
- **Worker:** the `availability` lane runs the schedule, catches up one missed read, records freshness (`nfl.availability`) and retries a failure after 30 minutes. `arbit-worker availability-capture` reads once on demand.
- **Tests:** 17 in `libs/arbit-data/tests/test_nfl_availability.py` and 2 lane tests in `services/worker/tests/test_daemon.py`.

**Open:**
- **Read gaps (R-066):** the PC is put to sleep from the Start menu most nights, which pauses every read. Under OWN-065 a Windows task now wakes it for each game window and the daily reads ([how](../../engineering/windows-wake.md)). The first real timer wake is still to be checked.
- **Release:** the lane reaches the running worker only on the owner's request.
- **Slice 3:** reads these captures by decision time, and adds their fields to the field catalog.

## Slices 3–7 in brief

- **Slice 3 features.** Team environment, from shrunk team ratings and pace:
  - dropback rate and pass rate over expected;
  - EPA and success rate;
  - drives, red-zone trips and finishing;
  - rest, home, roof and surface;
  - expected QB.

  Opponent: defense and pass rush (PFR pressures, sack rate), coverage tendencies from earlier seasons. Availability: report and practice status, roster status, depth rank, recent snaps, and teammates' and opponents' absences. Role: snap, route proxy, target, air yards, carry, red-zone and goal-line shares. Efficiency: against nflverse's expected values (CP and CPOE, xYAC, RYOE, EP), shrunk with measured constants. Cold-start priors: position, depth rank, draft capital, combine testing, age and experience. Every feature is computed as of a game's kickoff through a reader that applies ADR-007, and is tested by changing every row published after it. Without game-day inactives (R-064), availability rests on injury and practice reports, depth charts and roster moves. Additions from the 2026-11-22 decisions:
  - **Defense against the position (OWN-068):** FFA points and opportunities allowed to each position, shrunk and adjusted for the offenses faced.
  - **Gap-aware reader (ADR-007 A1, A3):** it labels each value's basis (`observed`, `source-timestamp` or `retrospective-rule`) and reads capture gaps from import history and catch-up receipts.
  - **Catalog re-review (A2):** exclusions made only for timing become applicability rules, and policy exclusions stay.
  - **Betting lines (OWN-067):** the Q-014 research note is written here. Lines are captured point in time but stay out of Baseline A.
- **Slice 3 order (2026-11-23).** Each part is its own governed model-class task:
  - **3a, implemented 2026-11-23 (TASK-20261123-001, ENG-053; not merged):** the point-in-time reader and the catalog re-review. See the [audit](../../research/point-in-time-audit-2026-11-23.md).
    - `arbit_model.point_in_time`, `timing` and `vintages` read 23 registered table patterns, across 20 families, as of any decision time.
    - Rows are taken from the version Arbit held, or admitted after a gap by A1.
    - Each row is labeled with its basis, and each reading carries a lineage record.
    - Fields excluded by policy are refused unless their gate is named.
    - The mutation test passes at five decision times. On real history, 56 table reads at four decision times returned no row known at or after its decision.
    - Catalog (A2): 38 fields become `as_imported`, observed weather becomes history, and betting lines move to the OWN-067 gate. The remaining exclusions are pinned as policy.
  - **3a follow-up, 2026-11-23 (TASK-20261123-002, ENG-054, OWN-072):**
    - The reader works on whole columns, with identical audit results.
    - The audit now takes 8.8 s instead of 34 s, and a whole season of decision times reads in 1.5 s (R-071 resolved).
    - The owner's timing order, held data first, is ADR-007 A5.
  - **3a follow-up, injuries (TASK-20261123-003, OWN-073, ENG-055):** `arbit_model.availability`. On game day ESPN's current status is final; otherwise the freshest copy of NFL's injury report; otherwise unknown. 3c builds availability features on it.
  - **3b, implemented 2026-11-23 (TASK-20261123-004, ENG-056; [note](../../research/team-features-2026-11-23.md)):** team environment and opponent, including defense against the position (OWN-068). That means FFA points and opportunities allowed to each position, shrunk and adjusted for the offenses faced, plus D/ST points allowed by the opponent's offense.
    - 65 specified features.
    - A mutation test.
    - Constants measured: adjusted games for half signal are RB 20.0, QB 22.2, TE 40.1, WR 42.0, K 55.3, and 9.1 for D/ST points an offense gives up.
    - Deferred to a follow-up: play-by-play rates (pass rate over expected, success, drives, red zone) with their own measured constants.
  - **DvP research, M3-3 continuation (TASK-20261123-005, OWN-074, ENG-057; [runbook](../../research/dvp/README.md)):** the owner's specification v1.1 governs defense against the position from here. Phases 0-1 are done: v0 reproduced exactly, the projection firewall, the completed-game observations (5,278 team-games), tests T01-T07 and T29-T40. On validated observations, k is about 9 for D/ST and 20-30 for QB and RB, while WR, TE and K are weak and can't be ranked. Next: Phase 2 (A00, A02-A10, A12), then the walk-forward against the offense-only B00. The 3b features keep v0's constants until Phase B decides.
  - **3c:** availability and role: report and practice status, roster status, depth rank, recent snaps, teammates' and opponents' absences, and snap, route, target, air-yard, carry and red-zone shares.
  - **3d:** efficiency against nflverse's expected values, shrunk with measured constants, and cold-start priors.
  - **3e:** the Q-014 research note (OWN-067): how lines are made, and the test plan. The ablation itself is slice 4.
  - **Beside it:** weather W1 (venue table, forecast record).
- **Slice 4 baselines and simulator.**
  - Baseline B: prior season or positional replacement.
  - Baseline A: empirical-Bayes shares and rates times team environment times availability.
  - Simulator: team volume, allocation among teammates, efficiency draws, kicker attempts by distance from drive endings, and D/ST from the opponent's simulated offense, all scored by the shared engine.

  Verified by exhaustive checks on small cases, and by the athlete mean equalling the score of the mean line. The release gate ADR is adopted before evaluation. Backtest: rolling origin by week over earlier seasons; holdout of the latest complete season; 2026 decided weeks as a forward test.
- **Slice 5 release.** Registry, pinned inputs, worker step after each nflverse import, freshness and the member API.
- **Slice 6 rest of season.** Sum of weekly distributions over FFA's remaining scoring periods, with byes and availability decay; rankings; replacement value from FFA's actual slots.
- **Slice 7 candidates.** Gradient-boosted component models (count objectives, season-grouped validation), hierarchical team ratings, and the direct-points challenger. Promotion is per position, through the gate.

## Evidence and outcome

**Slice 1:** see above.

**Architecture research** ([note](../../research/ffa-projection-methods-2026-11-09.md), [receipt](../../research/ffa-signal-study-2026-11-09.json)). FFA stat lines for 2016–2025 were scored by the shared engine. Opportunity carries half its signal within two games; efficiency and touchdowns need dozens. Touchdowns are 30–45% of skill-position variance. D/ST and kickers are driven by the team environment and the opponent. The design follows in ADR-006, accepted by the owner on 2026-11-09 (OWN-062).
